Number & Pattern

HOW TO PLAY

Click Start Challenge to initialize your timed 20-minute session.
Select one of the 4 options using your mouse or keyboard shortcuts (1-4 / A-D).
Use the Question Palette to jump directly to any question at any moment.
Submit when finished, or let the timer auto-submit at 00:00 for instant review.
